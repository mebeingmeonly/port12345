# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Riya Gupta --- Personal Portfolio Website

## 1. Document Overview

### Product Name

Riya Gupta Personal Portfolio

### Product Type

Personal professional portfolio website

### Primary Objective

Create a portfolio website that positions Riya Gupta as a content
strategist, scriptwriter, personal branding specialist, and creative
thinker.

The website should communicate her experience, demonstrate her strategic
approach to content, showcase measurable outcomes, and provide a clear
way for potential clients, founders, creators, and brands to contact
her.

### Primary Audience

-   Founders
-   Creators
-   Podcasters
-   Personal brands
-   Marketing teams
-   Businesses seeking content strategy and execution support
-   Potential collaborators and employers

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Background

Riya Gupta works across content strategy, scriptwriting, personal
branding, creative direction, social media management, and AI-assisted
content workflows.

Her experience includes working with creators, podcasters, founders, and
brands. Her work has involved developing content ideas, writing scripts,
managing content calendars, conducting research, coordinating with
creative teams, and evaluating content performance.

The website should present her professional experience through clear
positioning, selected work, case studies, measurable results, and
insights into her content-thinking process.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Product Goals

### Primary Goals

1.  Establish Riya's professional positioning in content strategy and
    creative content.
2.  Showcase selected projects and documented outcomes.
3.  Explain the services she can provide.
4.  Demonstrate her approach to content research, ideation, scripting,
    and optimization.
5.  Build credibility through relevant experience, metrics, and
    testimonials.
6.  Encourage qualified visitors to contact her.
7.  Provide a platform for publishing content insights and personal
    brand material.

### Secondary Goals

1.  Present her interest in AI and content workflows.
2.  Support future personal brand growth.
3.  Create a central location for professional information and work
    samples.
4.  Make it easy to update projects, insights, and achievements over
    time.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Non-Goals

The initial product does not need to include:

-   A full client-management system
-   An e-commerce store
-   A complex user account system
-   A public marketplace
-   Unverified performance claims
-   Content or testimonials published without permission
-   Advanced analytics dashboards for external visitors

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Core Positioning

The website should position Riya as someone who:

-   Thinks strategically about content.
-   Understands hooks, audience attention, and content structure.
-   Can write and develop content for multiple platforms.
-   Works across creators, founders, podcasters, and brands.
-   Uses performance data to learn and improve.
-   Explores AI as a productivity and research aid while retaining human
    judgment.
-   Connects creative execution with broader content and business
    objectives.

### Suggested Positioning Statement

Riya Gupta is a content strategist and scriptwriter who helps creators,
founders, and brands develop intentional content through research,
creative thinking, personal branding, and content execution.

## 7.8 Content Creation Process Section

### Purpose

Explain how Riya approaches a project from research through improvement.

### Process Stages

#### Stage 1: Research

Understand the audience, topic, platform, and objective.

#### Stage 2: Identify the Angle

Find a relevant perspective, problem, insight, or hook.

#### Stage 3: Build the Structure

Develop the narrative, script, content flow, and call to action.

#### Stage 4: Create and Coordinate

Collaborate with writers, editors, designers, clients, or other
stakeholders.

#### Stage 5: Review and Improve

Evaluate available performance data and identify opportunities for
iteration.

### Acceptance Criteria

-   The steps are understandable to potential clients.
